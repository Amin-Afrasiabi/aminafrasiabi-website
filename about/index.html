About Amin Afrasiabi, MD

Pediatric urologist, academic researcher, and medical educator with a clinical focus on congenital and reconstructive pediatric urology.

Amin Afrasiabi, MD (full name Mohammad Amin Afrasiabi; publications may appear as MA Afrasiabi) is a pediatric urologist and academic researcher affiliated with Tehran University of Medical Sciences and Children's Medical Center in Tehran.

His clinical work focuses on congenital urinary tract disorders, reconstructive pediatric urology, upper and lower urinary tract function, and long-term outcomes after complex pediatric urologic surgery.

Clinical focus

Congenital urinary tract disorders

CAKUT, hydronephrosis and UPJO, vesicoureteral reflux, posterior urethral valves, and duplex systems.

Reconstructive pediatric urology

Hypospadias, continence surgery, complex urinary reconstruction, and long-term functional follow-up.

Academic medicine

Prospective registries, outcomes research, systematic evidence synthesis, and responsible use of artificial intelligence in pediatric urology.

Education

He founded Pediatric Urology Academy as an evidence-based educational initiative for pediatric urology, integrating guidelines, primary literature, clinical reasoning, and surgical judgment.
