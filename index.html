Clinical focus

Complex pediatric urology, approached with precision.

Congenital disease, reconstruction, lower urinary tract function, and long-term outcomes are considered as connected clinical problems rather than isolated diagnoses.

Congenital Urology / CAKUT

Congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract, including duplex systems and related anatomic variants.

Upper Urinary Tract & Hydronephrosis

Hydronephrosis and ureteropelvic junction obstruction (UPJO), with emphasis on anatomy, function, timing, and outcomes.

Vesicoureteral Reflux & Lower Urinary Tract Dysfunction

Reflux, bladder dysfunction, posterior urethral valves (PUV), pressure management, continence, and upper-tract preservation.

Hypospadias & Genital Reconstruction

Primary and reoperative reconstruction with attention to long-term functional and cosmetic outcomes.

Complex Pediatric Urologic Reconstruction

Complex urinary reconstruction and continence procedures requiring integrated anatomic and functional decision-making.
