Research

Clinical research in pediatric urology

Research focus areas of Amin Afrasiabi, MD (Mohammad Amin Afrasiabi; MA Afrasiabi) in congenital and reconstructive pediatric urology, clinical outcomes, evidence synthesis, and medical artificial intelligence.

Research focus areas

These areas describe research focus and the questions under study. They are not a list of published findings; indexed publications are on the Publications page.

01

Hydronephrosis and ureteropelvic junction obstruction (UPJO)

Natural history and prediction in pediatric hydronephrosis and UPJO, with emphasis on anatomy, function, timing, and outcomes.

Education: Pediatric Urology Academy, UPJO season

02

Posterior urethral valves (PUV)

Posterior urethral valves and associated bladder dysfunction, with attention to pressure management, continence, and upper-tract preservation.

03

Pediatric reconstructive urology

Primary and reoperative hypospadias repair, complex urinary reconstruction, and continence procedures, with attention to long-term functional and cosmetic outcomes.

Education: Pediatric Urology Academy, hypospadias season

04

Clinical outcomes and prospective registries

Structured longitudinal datasets designed around clinically meaningful decision points, with functional, renal, surgical, and quality-of-care outcomes after conservative and operative management.

05

Evidence synthesis

Systematic review and critical appraisal aimed at separating established practice from uncertainty and expert judgment.

06

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

Clinically grounded machine learning and AI for prediction, phenotyping, and decision support in pediatric urology, applied responsibly.
