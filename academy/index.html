Pediatric Urology Academy

An evidence-based educational initiative founded by Amin Afrasiabi, MD.

Channels Watch Listen Episodes LinkedIn Instagram

Pediatric Urology Academy is designed as a structured educational platform for pediatric urology, translating guidelines, primary literature, clinical reasoning, and operative judgment into concise teaching.

The intended audience includes pediatric urologists, urologists, pediatric surgeons, fellows, residents, and researchers who need clinically precise education without oversimplifying uncertainty.
